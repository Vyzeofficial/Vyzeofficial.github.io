About SyraTHS

SyraTHS is a set of free tools for reading laptop listings. A listing is written to sell the laptop. These tools show you what it says, what it leaves out, and whether the laptop can run the games and programs you care about.

What is on the site

  • Decoder explains a spec or a phrase from a listing in plain words.
  • Checker reads a pasted listing and points out claims that are vague, inflated or missing, with the reason for each.
  • Can it run? compares a laptop with the listed requirements of 50 games and 50 programs.
  • Top laptops lists laptops that review sites rated well for school, coding, creative work, gaming and travel.
  • Evidence collects court cases, regulator findings and reviewer tests that show misleading listings are common.
  • My laptops keeps a private list of your own laptops if you choose to sign in.

Where the information comes from

We do not test laptops ourselves. Each pick on Top laptops was named by a review site, and the price and specs come from the review linked in the small print under it.

Game and program requirements come from each publisher's own page. How strong a processor or graphics card is comes from PassMark benchmark scores, and some of those scores are estimates. Every entry shows its source.

How the site pays for itself

It does not. SyraTHS does not sell laptops, earns nothing when you click a store link, and shows no ads. Buy buttons go to the store's own page or to a search for that model. We do not use analytics or tracking. The Privacy policy lists the few places data does move.

What the tools cannot do

Their answers are estimates. A benchmark score tells you how your hardware compares on paper, not what frame rate you will get, and Can it run? does not check your operating system or free disk space. The Checker reads the words in a listing. It cannot tell whether those words are true, so read reviews of the exact model before you buy.
