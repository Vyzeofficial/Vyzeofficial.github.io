What each price range should get you

If a listing costs noticeably less than its tier without a real sale attached, something on this list was cut. Find out what.

$400–600

Entry tier. Fine as a second machine or for basic school use. Expect a dim low-res screen, slow storage, and soldered RAM.

$700–900

Everyday tier done properly. The sweet spot for most students, and where price-per-performance peaks.

$1000–1500

Performance tier, or a premium everyday build with a better screen and battery. Watch for money spent on thinness instead of speed.

$1800+

Enthusiast tier or specialized work. At this price, weak cooling or 16GB RAM is a red flag, not a trade-off.
