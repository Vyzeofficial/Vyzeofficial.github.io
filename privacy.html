Privacy policy

Short version: you can use almost all of SyraTHS without giving us anything. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for the My laptops page. There are no ads and no tracking. Here is where data does move.

Last updated October 6, 2026

What we collect

If you only read the site, Checker and Decoder included, nothing about you, unless you choose to answer a "Was this right?" question (see below). We do not run analytics, we do not show ads, and we do not set cookies for tracking.

Signing in with Google (My laptops)

The My laptops page lets you sign in with your Google account. Google tells our sign-in service your name, email address and a unique account ID. We use that to show you who is signed in and to keep your list apart from everyone else's. We never see your Google password and we never post to your account.

Anything you add to the page is stored with that account ID: the laptop name, processor, graphics, memory, storage, screen and any notes you write. The page is private. Only you can read your entries, and you can edit or delete any of them whenever you like. We do not show them to other visitors, sell them or use them for ads. Please keep personal details such as serial numbers and addresses out of the notes.

Sign-in and storage are provided by Google Firebase, which processes this data for us under Google's terms. Firebase keeps a sign-in token in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. It is needed for the account to work and is not used for tracking. Signing out removes it. To have your account data deleted, delete your entries on the My laptops page, or ask through the report form on the Evidence page and we will remove everything stored under your account.

The listing checker and the decoder

Text you paste into the Listing checker and terms you type into the Decoder are processed by code running in your own browser. They are not sent to us and they are not saved, unless you send feedback with the listing included. When you open a title from the checker's "What this laptop can run" list, the listing is kept in your browser's own storage for a moment so Can it run? can read it. It is deleted as soon as that page opens it.

"Was this right?" feedback

Results on the Listing checker and Can it run? end with a "Was this right?" question. Answering is optional. If you answer, we store which page it was, what was checked (for example "valorant (can't run)"), your yes or no, any note you type, and the time. If you answer no, there is a box, ticked by default, that also sends the listing text or laptop specs you checked, so we can see what went wrong. Untick it to leave the listing out. We do not store your name, account, IP address or anything else about you with the answer.

Feedback is stored in the site's Google Firebase database, and only the site owner can read it. On copies of the site that are not connected to it, nothing is sent.

The report form

The form on the Evidence page is the other place where we store anything you type. If you send a report, we keep the link you entered (if any) and the text you wrote, along with the time you sent it. We do not ask who you are and the form does not need a sign-in. Only the site owner can read reports. A report is not published automatically, and we may use what it describes to improve the site. Please leave out personal details such as your name, address or order numbers. If you want a report removed, say so through the same form and describe which one it was.

Reports are stored only on the copy of the site that is hosted with Claude. On other copies of the site the form does not save anything.

Fonts and other services

Every page loads its typefaces (Inter and Chakra Petch) from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details, the same as for any site that uses Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles that.

Where this site is hosted (for example Claude or GitHub Pages), the host may keep ordinary server logs. We do not control those and we do not see them.

Links to other sites

Store buttons and source links take you to sites we do not run. Their owners decide what they collect, and their own policies apply once you click through. We earn nothing from those clicks.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Questions

To ask about this policy, use the report form on the Evidence page or email syrathsdev@gmail.com.

SyraTHS does not sell laptops, and it earns nothing when you click a store link. Buy buttons go to the store's own page or a search for that model. No ads and no tracking. Signing in is optional.
