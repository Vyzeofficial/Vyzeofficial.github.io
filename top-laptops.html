How we chose these laptops

We do not test laptops ourselves. Each pick is a laptop that a review site named as the best for that use, such as PCWorld or Tom's Hardware. The price and specs on each card come from the review linked in its small print.

We sorted every laptop by its listed price and left out any recommendation we could not find a US price for. A laptop shows up under more than one use when several reviewers named it.
